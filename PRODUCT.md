# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

People deciding whether to work with Stuti Singh: hiring leads, collaborators, and founders who open this on a laptop before a call. They want to see how the work behaves, not a list of claims.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio of the non-social work: tools and interfaces that decide what stays visible and what should disappear. Success is a visitor trying a redaction, reading a product surface, and knowing what she actually builds.

## Brand Personality

Smooth, precise, sunlit. Three words: measured, lucid, tactile. Confidence without a pitch.

## Anti-references

Developer terminal portfolios, cream editorial landing pages, icon-card grids, SaaS metric heroes, and a dark clone of Arkandis Labs.

## Design Principles

Show the behavior. Keep identity useful after it is removed. One instrument per section. Sample data stays labeled as sample.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for body text, visible focus, keyboard access to every demo, and reduced motion that leaves content in place.
